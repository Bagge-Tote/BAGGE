Our Story

A tote is just fabric until someone gives it a story.

BAGGE started with a simple idea: the bag you carry every day shouldn't be blank. Since 2026 we've been printing cotton canvas totes — fruit, phrases, hearts, and your own words or photos — because what you carry should say something about you.

Every bagge is cut, sewn and hand-printed in small batches, starting at ₹199. No two press runs are identical, which is exactly how we like it.
